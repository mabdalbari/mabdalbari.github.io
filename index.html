About

Clinical practice meets data engineering.

Most healthcare data work is done by people who have never filled a prescription. Most clinical work is done by people who have never modeled a dataset. I have done both.

EST. 2018
TANTA · CAIRO

I now lead digital transformation across 27 health centres at the Egyptian Ministry of Health, building the reporting infrastructure, forecasting models, and governance frameworks that were absent during my clinical years.
