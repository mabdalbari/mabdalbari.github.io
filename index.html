Selected work

Selected projects.

From raw data to executive insight — each project covers the full pipeline.

HealthcarePower BI · SSIS · SQL

Egypt Doctors Dashboard

Raw physician records from multiple field sources were ingested, cleaned, and modelled in PowerBI warehouse via powerquery ETL pipelines. The final Power BI dashboard surfaces specialty distribution, geographic coverage gaps across Egypt’s medical workforce of 16,770 physicians, giving health planners a clear, actionable view from a single source of truth.

Supply ChainPower Query · Power BI

Drug Warehouses of Egypt

Raw distributor and warehouse data from multiple national sources was collected, structured, and transformed with Power Query. The resulting Power BI directory enables pharmacists and supply chain managers to locate stock across 1,900+ facilities by region, specialty, and contact channel, converting a fragmented dataset into an operational decision tool.

Labour MarketPower BI · DAX

Medical Sales Skills Atlas

Raw job posting data was extracted from the top medical sales roles on LinkedIn Egypt and structured for analysis. Skills were categorised and weighted using Power BI and DAX, producing an atlas that maps the competencies most demanded by employers in the MENA pharmaceutical sales market. The insight equips pharmacy graduates with a data-backed roadmap into the industry.

Retail AnalyticsSQL Server · SSIS · Power BI

Canada Multi-Category Store

The full BI pipeline was built from scratch as the ITI capstone: entity-relationship design and relational modelling in SQL Server, SSIS ETL to load and transform a Canadian multi-category retail dataset, and a Power BI executive dashboard delivering sales-to-profit analysis, margin drivers, and category performance hierarchies, demonstrating the complete journey from raw data to boardroom insight.
